## Sound effects: `sfx/`

All SFX were synthesized from scratch with numpy in `scripts/make_sfx.py`, using no samples.
They are original works dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), so no attribution is required.

| File | Description | Used in bed at (s) |
|---|---|---|
| `sfx/impact_big.wav` | sub boom, low thump, transient crack and synthetic hall tail (4.5 s) | 0.0, 182.48, 296.00 (final hit) |
| `sfx/impact_soft.wav` | lighter boom (3.5 s) | 76.16, 272.00 |
| `sfx/riser_6s.wav` | noise and pitch-glide riser with accelerating tremolo; ends abruptly on the downbeat | 71.88–76.16, 177.21–182.48 (tail cut to 2 bars) |
| `sfx/riser_4s.wav` | same design, 4 s | 268.0–272.0 |
| `sfx/whoosh.wav` | band-swept noise whoosh that pans left to right and peaks at 62% of its length (1.6 s) | 149.84–151.44 (peak 150.83) |
| `sfx/reverse_swell_2s.wav` | reverse-cymbal style metallic swell (2 s) | 180.48–182.48 |
| `sfx/tape_stop.wav` | standalone synth "power-down" tape stop (1.2 s) | not used. The 19.77–20.67 s tape stop is DSP applied to Exhilarate itself |

Freesound.org was not used because its CC0 originals need a login to download.
