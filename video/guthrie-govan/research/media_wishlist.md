# Media wishlist: Guthrie Govan documentary (Turkish, 300 s)

This list covers every segment in `script.json`. Items are in priority order: **P1** must have, **P2** nice to have, **P3** optional or fallback.

**Licence notes**
- Wikimedia Commons files are free to use. Keep the attribution string.
- Album and magazine covers are copyrighted. They are used here as small, identifying illustrations in a private video. Take them from Wikipedia's non-free file pages, Discogs or the official label/band sites.
- Do **not** use film stills from *Dune* or artwork from *The Simpsons*. Text cards or generic stock work better.

### Search tips for the media collector
- **Album covers:** use Wikipedia non-free cover pages (e.g. `File:Erotic Cakes cover.jpg` on en.wikipedia), Discogs master pages, or Kscope and the Aristocrats' online store.
- **Magazine covers:** for *Guitar Techniques* and *Guitarist*, look at Future plc back-issue listings, magazinesdirect and eBay listings. Search "Guitar Techniques Guthrie Govan cover" and "Guitar Player July 2011 Guthrie Govan" (that issue ran a cover story on him).
- **Hand close-ups:** Govan keeps a low social-media profile (Guitar World, 2021). For close-up hand shots, prefer Commons crops or generic stock rather than scraping fan videos.
